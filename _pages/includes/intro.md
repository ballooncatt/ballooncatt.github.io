
# About Me

I am **Dongjie Fu (付栋杰)**, a second-year master's student at the School of Software Technology, [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/). My advisor is Professor Tao Jin (金涛), and I conduct my research in Professor Zhou Zhao (赵洲)'s lab. Before that, I received my B.E. degree from [Shandong University](https://www.sdu.edu.cn/).

**I am actively looking for job opportunities, feel free to drop me an email.**

My research focuses on **Speech / Audio Large Language Models** and **Audio-Visual Understanding**. I have published several papers as first author / co-first author in top-tier conferences and journals such as NeurIPS, EMNLP, ACM MM and TIP, with **120+ citations** on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=nLll-OIAAAAJ).

My current research topics include:
* Spoken Dialogue Systems / Audio Large Language Models
* Audio-Visual Speech Understanding / Sound Separation
