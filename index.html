About Me

I am a second-year M.S. student in Artificial Intelligence at the School of Software Technology, Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Tao Jin. I received my B.E. in Artificial Intelligence from the School of Computer Science and Technology, Shandong University in 2024, where I was a member of the IR Lab advised by Prof. Pengjie Ren.

My research focuses on Spoken Dialogue Systems, Audio Large Language Models, and Audio-Visual Understanding. I am especially interested in evaluation benchmarks for audio LLMs and in aligning them through reinforcement learning and on-policy distillation.

I have published 7+ papers as first / co-first author at top venues including NeurIPS, EMNLP, ACM MM, IEEE TIP, ICLR, KDD, CVPR, ACM TOIS, with 120+ citations on Google Scholar. I am currently a research intern at Tencent Hunyuan, working on speech dialogue large models.

Feel free to drop me an email if you'd like to chat about audio LLMs, dialogue systems, or potential collaborations.
